The TDCI is inviting to the side-event “Shared Data, Shared Action: Tracking gender-inclusion and multi-level collaborative efforts through open data” on 08th October, 2026, as part of the 9th edition of Transport and Climate Change Week.

Transport data is only as powerful as the trust and cooperation behind it. Political commitments to decarbonise transport are only as strong as the evidence that can hold them accountable, and building that evidence means connecting actors who don't always know each other, but each hold a piece of the picture. This session brings together three different scales of that ecosystem. A government voice, Dominican Republic, presenting the Ministerial Declaration on Transport Decarbonization launched at COP30 on behalf of the eleven endorsing countries, shows why data is the infrastructure behind political promises: national commitments only carry weight if they can be tracked and reported. A regional voice, the Centro de Movilidad Sostenible de Latinoamérica (CMS), shows how data is collected on the ground across Latin America, and what it takes to turn that evidence into something operational and locally owned. And a thematic voice, the Global Alliance for Feminist Transport, shows that even solid regional data can still leave people out unless it is disaggregated: data quality is not only technical, but also about who gets counted and whose needs shape policy. Together, we ask: what does it take to build trust, shared evidence, and cooperation across contexts, so that data becomes not just information, but a lever for inclusive, implementable transport decarbonisation? 

Transport and Climate Change Week is GIZ's annual flagship event on decarbonising transport, and serves as a hub for regional and international exchanges, featuring partner-hosted side events, fostering peer learning, and incorporating insights from experts. This year's conference will be virtual. Register and find further info on https://transportweek.org/
