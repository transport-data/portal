The TDCI is inviting to the side-event “Shared Data, Shared Action: Tracking gender-inclusion and multi-level collaborative efforts through open data” on 08th October, 2026, as part of the 9th edition of Transport and Climate Change Week.

Transport data is only as powerful as the trust and cooperation behind it. Political commitments to decarbonise transport are only as strong as the evidence that can hold them accountable, and building that evidence means connecting actors who don't always know each other, but each hold a piece of the picture. This session brings together three different scales of that ecosystem to ask the question: what does it take to build trust, shared evidence, and cooperation across contexts, so that data becomes not just information, but a lever for inclusive, implementable transport decarbonisation? 

Transport and Climate Change Week is GIZ's annual flagship event on decarbonising transport, and serves as a hub for regional and international exchanges, featuring partner-hosted side events, fostering peer learning, and incorporating insights from experts. This year's conference will be virtual. Register and find further info on [transportweek.org](https://transportweek.org/).
